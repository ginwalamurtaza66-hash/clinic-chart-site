Online booking

Patients book themselves. You still decide.

A booking page on your own address. Patients pick a time, you confirm or decline it, and the slot is held the moment they ask — so two people cannot take the same appointment.

01

They pick a real time

Your opening hours, your slot length, your holidays and half-days. Times already taken are struck out. Share the link on WhatsApp or as a QR code — there is no app for them to install and no account to create.

02

The slot is held immediately

Reserved the moment they submit, not when you get round to it. If someone else was mid-form for the same time, their booking is refused rather than quietly accepted for an appointment that has gone.

03

You confirm or decline

Requests appear on the clinic screen as they arrive, with a chime — nobody has to keep checking. Declining hands the time straight back to everyone else. Nothing is booked into your day without a person agreeing to it.

04

They keep hold of it

After booking they can save their appointment to WhatsApp, send it by SMS, or add it to their calendar, and reopen it later to check or cancel. A patient who cancels early gives the slot back automatically.

Every booking, kept

A full history with filters by status and date. Archive what you no longer need to look at rather than deleting it — the record of who asked for what, and when, is the only evidence if an appointment is ever disputed.

The booking page sees almost nothing

It can read your clinic name, your fee and which times are taken. It cannot read patients, visits, receipts or your other appointments, and there is no way to look up a booking by phone number — so nobody can discover who has an appointment with you. That is enforced by the database, not by the page.

Straight answers

Questions worth asking.

What happens to my records if I stop using Clinic Chart?

You keep them. The Firebase project belongs to your Google account, so we can't take it away — and the app has a full export that writes every patient, visit, prescription and receipt to a file you hold. Nothing about leaving requires our cooperation.

Is it compliant with India's data protection law?

Not yet, and we won't claim otherwise. The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 and the obligations that affect clinics take effect in May 2027. We're building towards them — consent capture, access logging, and the handling of patient requests — and we'll be straight with you about where we are. Anyone selling you clinic software that claims full compliance today is worth a second look.

What if two people edit the same patient at once?

Both versions are kept and you're shown them side by side to choose between. Nothing is silently overwritten. This matters most offline, when two devices can't see each other's changes until they reconnect.

Do I need internet to set it up?

For the initial setup, yes — the clinic's database has to be created and your staff accounts added. After that, day-to-day work doesn't need a connection.

How many patients can it handle?

Search covers your whole patient list however large it gets. Storage is the practical limit: 1 GB is comfortably several years for a clinic seeing thirty to a hundred patients a day. We'll size it honestly with you before you commit, based on your actual volume.

Can my receptionist use it without seeing clinical notes?

Yes. Receptionists can register patients and handle billing; clinical records and the AI assistant are restricted to doctors. The restriction is enforced by the database itself, not just hidden in the interface.

Can patients book appointments themselves?

Yes, from a booking page on your own address — no app to install and no account to create. The time is held the moment they submit, so the same slot cannot be given to two people, and nothing enters your day until you confirm it. Declining hands the time straight back.

Can someone look up another patient's appointment?

No. The booking page can read your clinic name, your fee and which times are taken, and nothing else — not patients, not visits, not your other appointments. There is deliberately no lookup by phone number, because that would let anyone type a number and discover who has an appointment with you. Patients reopen their own booking with a private reference.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Both, and on a desktop browser. It installs to the home screen like an app, with no app store and no separate download.
